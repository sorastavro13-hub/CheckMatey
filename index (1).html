Fair Play Centre

Good competition means following the rules, respecting opponents, and handling uncertainty responsibly.

✓
Play honestlyMake your own decisions and follow the rules of the platform you use.
↗
Report privatelyIf something concerns you, use the official reporting process on the service where the game happened.

Don't accuse publiclyA surprising move, a high accuracy score, or a loss alone does not prove cheating.
✦
Respect the personCritique decisions, not people. Congratulate good play and take breaks when frustrated.

CheckMatey does not detect cheating or judge other players. It is a learning-focused prototype, not an official fair-play authority.
